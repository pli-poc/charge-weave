# Business policies adopted by the semantic profile

These are explicit ChargeWeave design decisions. Primary protocol and regulator sources inform the requirements; they do not prescribe every canonical class or state name.

- **Complete snapshots:** cardinality, totals, duplicate effects and missing records are evaluated over a complete, authorized business snapshot. Partial API responses must be assembled before validation. Storage/query isolation and snapshot completeness remain runtime responsibilities.
- **Evidence:** references, flags and content digests retain auditable claims. The ontology verifies agreement between these claims; trusted services must establish their truth, signatures, timestamps and retention.
- **Contracts and exit:** agreement acceptance, obligation assessment and residual financial ownership are explicit. Closing an account does not cancel debt or retention duties. Contract amendments create successor versions.
- **State changes:** transitions are events with source evidence. Current snapshots agree with the subject state; chained transitions preserve target, property, previous/next state and nondecreasing event time. Terminal records are corrected by new evidence. Sequence uniqueness is not claimed for transition chains; process step sequence is independently constrained.
- **Metering and billing:** register changes/reset require a new epoch or justified estimate. A local debit CDR requires a ready assessment with usage, pricing, tax, authorization and responsibility resolved. Evidence flags are necessary semantic conditions, not a substitute for metrology or price calculation.
- **Corrections:** roaming Credit CDRs fully reverse the linked original monetary totals with matching parties/currency, following the reviewed OCPI profile. Invoice credit notes can be partial but cannot cumulatively exceed the original gross amount. Refunds, chargebacks, credits and record corrections remain distinct records. Published records must be immutable in persistence; RDF validation alone does not enforce historical immutability.
- **Money:** decimal arithmetic is used. Positions distinguish original liability, collections, credits, write-off and remaining balance. This profile treats remaining receivables as nonnegative; customer overpayments require a payable/refund record. A capture cannot be overallocated. Currency conversion is explicit; legal rounding and fiscal rules must be pinned by the runtime.
- **Allowances:** the period balance subtracts actual consumption, active reservations and expired value from opening rollover and grants. Amount-based allowances identify currency. Runtime atomicity must prevent competing reservations from overspending between validation and commit.
- **Settlement and reimbursement:** approval evidence and unresolved-difference counts govern settlement release. Home reimbursement approval binds claim, measured energy, rate, amount and currency; a paid claim cites a confirmed payout. These are model decisions, not instructions to move money.
- **Authorization and events:** operational permits require an active grant for the exact principal, scope, permission and decision time. Broader hierarchy expansion belongs to the authorization service. Duplicate event keys include tenant, producer and processing context; duplicate processing creates no new business effects. The event store must enforce atomic uniqueness and payload integrity.
- **Energy:** an override never removes safety or contractual hard limits. Export requires an applicable site agreement and validity window. Feasible-power evidence does not certify electrical installation or closed-loop control.
- **Privacy:** verified identity, response evidence, holds, purposes and safeguards are explicit. Legal basis, exceptions, deadlines by jurisdiction and actual erasure/export require controller decisions. An active hold blocks completed disposition in this profile.
- **Recovery and exit:** success records measured RTO/RPO results; completed migration reconciles records and records revocation of source credentials. Full restoration and revocation must be tested against actual infrastructure.

When business policy changes, change the requirement, model/rule, accepted/rejected cases and lifecycle table together. A metadata coverage check cannot replace reviewing whether a rule expresses the intended business decision.

## Additional 1.2.0 contracts

- **Historical authorization:** Current permits use enabled principals and current active grants. Historical permits require immutable decision-time evidence for the exact grant, permission and scope. The snapshot is captured at the decision time; later revocation does not rewrite it.
- **Sponsored benefits:** A different customer can consume a balance only with an explicit sponsorship identifying the funder, beneficiary, agreement, scoped customer/session and usage-time validity. Sponsorship does not transfer ownership of the funder's entire balance.
- **Accounting and corrections:** Closed periods retain a close timestamp and evidence. A late correction uses its original usage-time responsibility and a distinct period open at posting. That posting period may subsequently close. Corrections are linked to the original and correcting record; the original period contains the original usage time.
- **Collection and refund reconciliation:** Allocation-backed collection equals posted allocations. External collection requires explicit evidence. Confirmed refunds reduce the money available for remaining posted allocations. A paid invoice has a settled same-currency position covered by collections and credits, excluding write-offs. Credit notes are positive reductions.
- **Rounding and conversion:** Decimal rounding supports HalfUp, HalfEven, toward-zero Down and away-from-zero Up at scales 0–6. Signed corrections are supported. Conversion uses the quoted base-to-quote direction, retained unrounded product and explicit target rounding. This does not define every tariff calculation policy: component stepping, caps, grace periods and tax applicability remain separate declared inputs and runtime algorithms.
- **Time:** Tax determination records original taxable time. Offset-bearing price instants must agree with UTC and the pinned IANA database. Repeated hours require First/Second selection; nonexistent times are rejected. The IANA check supplements portable SHACL-SPARQL.
- **Settlement legs:** Each posted economic leg names different liable/entitled parties and a unique reference within its tenant and batch. Separate agreements and batches can represent intermediaries using the same usage record. No universal assumption equates wholesale and retail margins. Approval also inspects unresolved cases directly attached to the batch or its items.
- **Operational evidence:** Completed account/asset exits consider actual scoped grants and unfinished sessions. Explicit billing blockers affect readiness. Required process steps cannot be skipped, predecessors stay within the process and have lower sequence numbers. Cabinet capacity is checked across all simultaneously active allocations using half-open intervals.
- **Privacy closure:** Closing a request retains fulfilment/refusal and the necessary response evidence. A hold directly on a disposed record applies even when the disposition omits a `legalHold` link; a hold on one unrelated invoice does not become an account-wide hold.
